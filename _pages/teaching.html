--- layout: archive title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} My pedagogical approach is grounded in disability justice, alongside the work of femme scholars of color such as Leah Lakshmi Piepzna-Samarasinha, Karen L. Suyemoto, and Audre Lorde. Drawing on their insights, I teach relationally and in community with my students; I intentionally reject Western models that stigmatize emotion in the classroom and define teaching through hierarchical control, rather than collective meaning-making. Instead, I approach the classroom as a space of mutual translation, where knowledge is built through collaborative processes that allow for vulnerability and, with that, intellectual risk-taking. As an educator, it matters deeply to me that my students have power and agency over their learning; beyond structuring the classroom to create space for this and build community where this can be possible, I build individual relationships with each my students, many of which extend beyond the semester through continued mentorship.
